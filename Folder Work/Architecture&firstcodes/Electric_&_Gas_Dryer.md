# Electric Dryer and Gas Dryer Comparison

## Electric Dryer Operation

The electric dryer draws air from outside the cabinet through the available louvers and openings. This air passes through the electric heating element, where it is heated to the required temperature. The heated air then enters the drum, allowing moisture to evaporate from the clothes during the drying process. The humid air is extracted from the drum and passes through the lint filter and trap duct before being discharged through the exhaust duct.

---

## Gas Dryer Operation

The gas dryer draws air from outside the cabinet through the available louvers and openings. Heat is generated through gas combustion inside the combustion chamber. The heated air is mixed with fresh incoming air and directed through the gas diffuser before entering the drum. Once inside the drum, the heated airflow removes moisture from the clothes. The humid air then passes through the lint filter and trap duct before being expelled through the exhaust duct.

# Heat Control Comparison

## Electronic Dryer

The electronic dryer controls temperature using multiple sensing devices distributed throughout the airflow path. These devices include:

- Inlet Thermistor
- Outlet Thermistor
- Safety Thermostat
- Outlet Thermostat
- Hi-Limit Thermostat

These components allow continuous temperature monitoring and provide precise control of the drying process.

### Mechanical / Gas Dryer

The mechanical or gas dryer relies on thermostatic devices for temperature regulation and system protection. The main components include:

- Inlet Thermostat
- Safety Thermostat
- Outlet Thermostat
- Hi-Limit Thermostat

These devices provide temperature regulation and safety protection during operation.

# Moisture Sensing Comparison

## Electronic Dryer

The electronic dryer detects moisture levels directly using sensor rods installed inside the drum. These metal bars monitor the conductivity of the laundry load, allowing the control system to estimate remaining moisture and optimize drying time.

Main sensing component:

- Sensor Rods (Metal Bars)

## Mechanical / Gas Dryer

The mechanical or gas dryer does not directly measure moisture. Instead, it relies on time and temperature-based control methods to estimate drying progress.

Main control elements:

- Timer
- Safety Thermostat
- Outlet Thermostat

# Noise Parameters

Drying performance and cycle duration can be influenced by several external factors.

### Exhaust Restrictions

Restrictions in the exhaust system reduce airflow and increase drying time. Common causes include:

- Long exhaust ducts
- Ducts containing multiple elbows or bends
- Lint accumulation inside ducts
- Dirty lint filters
- Moisture recirculation

### Load Size

The size and distribution of the laundry load affect airflow and moisture removal efficiency. Conditions that may extend drying time include:

- Very large loads
- Bulky loads
- Single-item loads or very light loads

### Fabric Type

Different fabrics retain and release moisture at different rates. Drying prediction becomes more complex when multiple fabric types are mixed together.

Examples include:

- Synthetic fabrics
- Cotton
- Polyester/Cotton blends
- Lycra / Spandex
- Sportswear
- Mixed fabric loads

### Notes

- More restrictive exhaust systems increase drying time.
- Larger loads generally require longer drying times.
- Mixed fabric loads reduce moisture prediction accuracy.

# Dryer Process Flow

## Electric Dryer Process

The electric drying process begins when air enters the appliance from the surrounding environment. The incoming air passes through the electric heating element, where it is heated to the target temperature before entering the drum. Inside the drum, the heated air removes moisture from the clothes. The resulting humid air exits the drum, passes through the lint filter and trap duct, and is finally discharged through the exhaust system.

---

## Gas Dryer Process

The gas drying process begins with ambient air entering the appliance. Heat is generated inside the combustion chamber through gas combustion. The heated air is mixed with fresh air and routed through the gas diffuser before entering the drum. As the heated air circulates through the clothes, moisture evaporates and is carried out of the drum. The humid air passes through the lint filter and trap duct before being exhausted outside the appliance.
